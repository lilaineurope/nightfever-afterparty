# No Entiendes (28 Nov 2026) - page to create: /explore/no-entiendes

## Facts
- Haoui Montaug's travelling cabaret / talent revue "No Entiendes!" - Wednesday nights at Danceteria from 1982 (card for Wednesday 15 Dec 1982 and Wednesday 5 Jan 1983), with DJ Anita Sarko as co-host from c. 1984; second-anniversary celebration at Danceteria, 1984; a sixth-anniversary night at The World (254 East 2nd St), Friday 25 March (1988) "with co-host Anita Sarko"; also at the Palladium (Nelson Sullivan's tape).
- It featured a young Madonna and early Beastie Boys (Wikipedia: Haoui Montaug); Madonna sang "Everybody" at No Entiendes in 1982 (videos below).
- Haoui also staged "Jesus Christ Superstar: The Lip Sync Opera" at Danceteria in 1985 - see dean-johnson-jcs/.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haoui_Montaug ; Gallery 98 cards (below); Nelson Sullivan tape.
- Transcripts: no guest says "No Entiendes" (searched every episode).
