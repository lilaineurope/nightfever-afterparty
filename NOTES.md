# Colleen Weinstein and The Continental (28 Nov 2026)

## Colleen Weinstein  (page to create: /explore/colleen-weinstein)
- Who: nightclub designer; wife of Arthur Weinstein (1947-2008, photographer and club owner: Hurrah, the Jefferson, the Continental, The World). Arthur did the lighting, Colleen the design.
- Alive: yes - interviewed at home in her Chelsea Hotel apartment for PIN-UP 27 (Fall/Winter 2019/20); Eric Goode on Night Fever (S03E06, 2023-24) says she "still lives in the Chelsea Hotel". No death notice found. (Alive or died: Alive, checked 28 Nov 2026.)
- Clubs she designed (all from her own account in PIN-UP, by Whitney Mallett):
  - The Jefferson (1980) - her first: an after-hours in their 3,600 sq ft loft above the old theatre on East 14th Street, where they lived; vintage furniture, walls papered with rolls from Secondhand Rose. (Arthur didn't design Hurrah with her - that was when they met.)
  - The Continental (after the Jefferson) - an old garage; big hanging sheets of plexiglass after the film Blow-Up, making rooms along a centre aisle; opened at 2 am. Mick Jagger once turned up early and offered to sweep the floor.
  - Palladium (1985) - the Michael Todd Room (Andree Putman did the main space): she found Michael Todd's corporate seal in a desk drawer, hence the name; inspiration Beauty and the Beast; Ian Schrager insisted on keeping the decay.
  - Milk Bar, Seventh Avenue - all white like A Clockwork Orange, lit like a lightbox.
  - The World (mid-1980s, Avenue C at East 2nd Street, a former catering hall) - the Crystal Room with Philip LaVerne marble-effect wallpaper, silver leather bar, original chandelier, French-maid cocktail waitresses.
  - The Ritz (in Webster Hall) - picture courtesy of her.
  - Limelight redesign (c. 1988/89) - the entrance with huge animal topiaries (giraffes, rabbits, an elephant, a camel, some in velvet vests) and walls of peacock feathers that club kids steamed open in the kitchen, 20 kids for ten hours a day.
  - Palace de Beaute (now a Petco) - corsetting, Robert Toledo's canvases hung like awnings; Pedro Almodovar sent her a handwritten note.
  - A Philadelphia nightclub; a Lower East Side boutique (in a Japanese magazine).
- Chelsea Hotel: she and Arthur lived there; she still does.
- Timeline: 1980 the Jefferson (party for Colleen turns into the club, Daily Beast) -> the Continental (West 25th St garage) -> mid-1980s The World -> 1985 Palladium Michael Todd Room -> c. 1988-89 Limelight redesign -> 2008 Arthur dies -> 2019 PIN-UP interview at the Chelsea Hotel.

## The Continental  (club page to create: /explore/continental - check the slug against the Continental Baths, which is a different place)
- What: Arthur and Colleen Weinstein's illegal after-hours club after the Jefferson.
- Where: a garage on West 25th Street (Chelsea), in the same building as Eric Goode's first club, the "Club With No Name" (Eric, S03E06). Exact street number not found.
- When: from c. 1980-81 (Studio 54 had just closed, Feb 1980; the Jefferson was still open when Arthur began building it). Closing date not found. Followed by The World (1984).
- Who ran it: Arthur Weinstein (lighting, the door) and Colleen Weinstein (design).
- The room: big hanging plexiglass sheets after Blow-Up, rooms along a centre aisle; the back room was "the Loading Dock" - a cold concrete loading bay where trucks unloaded (Eric: "he was in the loading bay"). Opened at 2 am.
- Who came: uptown types after Studio 54 closed - tennis star Vitas Gerulaitis, Billy Idol (Bedford + Bowery / Nightclubbing); Mick Jagger, who arrived early and offered to sweep (Colleen, PIN-UP).
- The police story: Arthur paid off police and fire officials to keep the after-hours open ("I began paying them $500 a week"); after Steve Rubell and Ian Schrager came out of prison for tax evasion, Rubell approached him and Arthur agreed to wear a wire against corrupt cops and firemen. Eric Goode: "he brought down the Ninth and Tenth Precinct... we all thought Arthur was going to get shot by the police" (S03E06 00:13:32). Daily Beast, paywalled - only the opening lines and quoted snippets seen.
- Sources: https://bedfordandbowery.com/2012/10/nightclubbing-after-hours-1980/ ; https://archive.pinupmagazine.org/articles/interview-colleen-weinstein-nightclub-design ; https://www.thedailybeast.com/club-kids-and-crooked-cops-when-new-york-went-wild-after-hours ; Night Fever S03E06.
- Pictures: drawn header + phone crop + polaroid (Gemini, same style and sizes as the Jefferson), after 18:24. No photo of the building or the club found.
