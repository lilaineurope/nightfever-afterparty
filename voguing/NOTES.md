# Voguing (28 Nov 2026) - /explore/voguing

## Facts
- The dance of the Harlem ballroom houses: from the 1960s-70s balls (Patricia Field saw her first in a Harlem brownstone in the early 70s), poses "like a fashion model on the cover of Vogue" that, Luis says, connected faster and faster until they became a dance. Late 1980s: the ball kids at the Tunnel, the Garage, Red Zone and the Sound Factory; Chi Chi Valenti's "Nations" (Details, Oct 1988) and Donald Suggs's "Venus Envy" (Village Voice) were the first pieces about it; the first Love Ball (1989); Johnny Dynell's "Elements of Vogue" and Malcolm McLaren's "Deep in Vogue" (1989, with Willi Ninja) were the first vogue records; then Paris Is Burning (1990) and Madonna's "Vogue" (1990), with Jose and Luis Xtravaganza dancing.
- Sources: Night Fever S01E08, S02E01, S03E01, S06E03; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vogue_(dance)
