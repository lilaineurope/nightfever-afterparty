# The Love Ball (28 Nov 2026) - /explore/love-ball

## Facts
- The Love Ball: Susanne Bartsch's AIDS benefit at the Roseland Ballroom, New York, 10 May 1989 (date from Nelson Sullivan's tape title), presented with DIFFA (Design Industry Foundation Fighting AIDS). It brought the Harlem ball houses together with fashion: houses walked categories, designers and celebrities judged. Raised about $400,000 for DIFFA (Wikipedia; FIT).
- Judges/walkers on the show: Marc Jacobs, Andre Leon Talley and Leigh Bowery among the judges (Michelle Visage); Susanne opened with Willi Ninja and the House of Xtravaganza; Michelle walked with the House of Magnifique; Zaldy and Matthew Kasten's "House of Donna Karan" nude illusion bodysuits that turned humans into aliens; Mario Testino in drag with five supermodels (Susanne); Laurie Pike was MC (S02E03). Madonna was there and, the legend goes, first saw voguing there (James).
- Love Ball II, 1991 (Roseland, then Los Angeles / Paris Folies Bergere per some sources - "palais de l'amour", $750,000 in Paris per Susanne, S02E03 00:46:03); the two balls together raised over $2.5 million (Paper / CFDA). Love Ball III with the CFDA, 2019.
