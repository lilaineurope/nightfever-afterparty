## ticket-1.jpg - SUGGESTED (882x559)
- What: the real entry ticket for Joy Division at the Bains-Douches, 18 December 1979, 20h30, 40 F, no. 00194
  (the band's only concert in France; recorded and released as "Les Bains Douches 18 December 1979"). Club logo = the bathhouse facade.
- Printed by: "imp. le souterrain, nanterre" (printed on the ticket's edge). Designer unknown.
- Source: auction lot photo, Aguttes (Paris) -
  https://www.aguttes.com/en/lot/2ef1b234-2d74-4617-ae8c-920797b2d827/5612442a-712d-4118-89f5-92c26aa5c206
  Cropped out of the frame from the lot photo.
- Licence: none stated (auction catalogue photo). Same footing as the Gallery 98 dealer photos already on the site.
- Credit line: "Ticket: Joy Division at the Bains-Douches, 18 December 1979 - auction photo, Aguttes"
