# Don Hill's (28 Nov 2026) - page to create: /explore/don-hill-s (check the slug)

- What: live rock club at 511 Greenwich Street, corner of Spring Street (Hudson Square / west SoHo).
- Before it: the building was the Munson Diner, then Brian McNally's restaurant Canal Bar (Miss Guy remembers it as "certainly out of my price range"); Don took it over after Canal Bar closed and it sat quiet for about six months before Squeezebox (Miss Guy, S03E10).
- Years: 1993 - April 2011 (Hudson Square BID history; amNY quotes the Ear Inn's owner that Hill opened it in 1993).
- Owner: Don Hill - a music-business man who ran it as a rock club; he gave Squeezebox "full freedom to do whatever we wanted and full support" (Miss Guy) and "just let me do whatever the hell I wanted" (Michael Schmidt). Died suddenly in early 2011, aged 66; a few months earlier he had sold the club to Nur Khan and Paul Sevigny. The club closed for good in April 2011, weeks after his death (last call the Monday after a Tribeca Film Festival after-party).
- Nights: Squeezebox (Fridays, from April 1994 per Miss Guy - Michael says 1993; to c. 2001), Michael Schmidt and Miss Guy, Mistress Formika hosting, Howie Pyro the first DJ, drag queens singing with a live punk band; Rock 'n' Roll Fag Bar 2008, Dean Johnson's memorial benefit (30 May 2008); Nur Khan's rock nights in the last years (bands and fashion-week parties).
- After: reopened as The Hills NYC (2014, NY Press / BrooklynVegan).
- Squeezebox regulars mentioned on the show: Debbie Harry, John Waters, Drew Barrymore, Juliette Lewis, Joan Jett, Jayne County (Miss Guy / James, S03E10 00:40:20).
